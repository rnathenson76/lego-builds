The sheet is generated by `tools/proportion_sheet.py`. The grid is 4 studs by

## 1. Units and scale

| Unit | Model | Real at 1:24 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 stud | 8 mm | 19.2 cm |
| 1 plate | 3.2 mm | 7.7 cm |
| 1 brick (3 plates) | 9.6 mm | 23 cm |
| Tire 2696, Ø 43.2 mm | Ø 5.4 studs (6.75 plates radius) | 11R22.5 tire, about 1.05 m |

The wheel parts come from the LDraw library: 2695 is the rim and 2696 is the
tire.

- **Tire width:** 12.8 mm, about 1.6 studs. A **dual pair** takes about 3.4
  studs.
- **Rim hub:** a round center hole. Wheels spin freely on a Technic axle or pin.
  The steer wheels mount on pins in steering knuckles.
- **Axle height:** the axle centers sit 6.75 plates (21.6 mm) off the ground.

### Clearances checked on paper

- **Cab to reefer unit:** about 6 studs. That leaves room to tilt the cab and to
  turn the trailer. The reefer unit's corner swings on a radius of about 10 studs
  around the kingpin.

- **Between the inner duals:** 5 studs of free space for the 4-wide frame and
  the walking-beam pivot.

## 4. Mechanisms: how each one fits

- **Steering:** a gear rack sits in front of the steer axle. A vertical shaft
  runs up through the cab's back wall to a knob on the Aerodyne roof (a "hand of
  god" control). The shaft has to split, or disengage, where the cab tilts;
  I'll prototype that in Phase 2.
- **Tilting cab:** the hinge axis sits at the front of the frame, at about x 2
  and 12 plates high. The cab latches at the rear onto the frame.
- **Walking beam:** the two drive axles sit on one beam per side, pivoting at
  x 27.5.
- **Fifth wheel and kingpin:** a turntable or pin, so the trailer lifts
  straight off.
- **Landing gear:** legs that slide or swing down.

## 5. Build notes for Phases 2–3

- **Trailer walls:** each side is 72 × 12 bricks. As SNOT tiles, that is
  roughly 36 plates of height × 72 studs per side. Phase 3 will give exact tile
  counts. Rough estimate: **more than 250 tiles per side**, depending on
  the mix of tile sizes.
- **Cab front:** a flat cabover face with the grille, turn signals and
  headlights (dual rectangular headlights on the 1978 K100).
